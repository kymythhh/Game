HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS

🎮 CONTROLS REFERENCE

  • Player 1: [W, A, S, D] keys for movement & actions.
  • Player 2: [Arrow Keys] and [Enter/Shift] for actions.
  • Gamepads: Supported natively! Press [Select] to swap controller ports.

⚡ ARCADE DUEL MINI-GAMES

  • Green Light Reflex: Strike your action button as soon as the screen flashes GREEN. False start gives point to opponent!
  • Tug-of-War Clash: Mash your button continuously to pull the rope past your goal line.
  • Pattern Mash: Match the 5-step arrow sequence faster than your rival.

🐍 REVERSE SNAKE SHOWDOWN

  • Player 1 (Snake): Hunt the fruit and cut off lanes. Stunned if you crash into yourself or hit traps.
  • Player 2 (Fruit): Evade the snake until the timer runs out! Collect blue orbs to drop purple traps.
